D-48 Staff PINs are **4 digits**, set by the owner and permanent (no first-login change). Supabase Auth refuses passwords shorter than 6, so the password Auth stores is `HMAC-SHA256(PIN_PEPPER, "pin:" + username + ":" + PIN)`; `PIN_PEPPER` is a server-only secret (32+ characters) that must never change once staff exist. Weak PINs are refused: 0000-9999 repeats, runs (1234, 7890, 4321), pairs (1212, 1122), years 1940-2030, and a few keypad favourites. Five wrong PINs lock the username for **1 hour** (30 per address per hour); the owner can unlock early. Trade-offs the owner accepted: the owner knows every PIN, so the activity log proves which login acted, not which person; anyone who knows a username can lock that person out for an hour. Older 6-digit PINs keep working (stored as typed) until the owner sets a 4-digit PIN for that person.

D-65 **Task Master design (Stage 3a, 23 Nov 2026).** (1) Workflow: created, assigned, accepted, in progress, submitted, in verification, completed; a rejected submission is "returned" and goes back to the assignee, who starts the correction and resubmits; the Owner can cancel an unfinished task. All transitions are database functions that check the caller and the current state under a row lock, and repeating an action (double tap, retry) returns quietly with no second history line. (2) Permissions: `tasks.view_own` (carried by Junior and Senior), `tasks.manage` (Owner only: create, assign, re-date, cancel, schedules, change requests, all-tasks and team views) and `tasks.verify` (D-53: the Owner, and Seniors the owner names with a personal grant; the Senior template never carries it and a Junior can never verify). Nobody verifies their own submission, the Owner included. (3) A verifier sees only tasks waiting in their queue or ones they verified; the assignee sees their own; the Owner sees all; anyone else gets the same answer as "no such task". (4) Staff cannot change ownership or deadline: they ask (reassign or new deadline) with a reason, one request at a time, and the Owner approves or refuses; a refusal needs a reason. (5) Overdue means a deadline before today (Pakistan time) on work not yet handed in; work waiting for verification is not overdue. (6) A staff member's list is ordered: overdue, urgent, deadline within two days, other pending work, returned for correction, then work waiting on verification. (7) Completion rate: tasks completed and verified on or before their deadline divided by tasks due in the period for that person, over 7, 30 or 90 complete days ending yesterday; cancelled tasks, unassigned tasks and tasks created after their own deadline are left out. (8) Schedules (D-56): a schedule's cycle makes exactly one task, enforced by a unique key; the daily job (00:05 Pakistan time, pg_cron) and the Owner's "Generate now" run the same function; the Nth week of a month is the Nth block of seven days (week 3 = days 15 to 21, due on the 21st; week 5 = day 29 to month end, absent in a 28-day February); a default person who has lost their login leaves the generated task unassigned rather than failing. (9) Evidence is none, a written note or a tick only (D-57). (10) Every task action is also written to the audit log; task history and comments are append-only.
