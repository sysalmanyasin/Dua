## Audit 3.0, slice F2: counting calculator — 22 Nov 2026
Plan: the "Audit 3.0 plan" doc, item P1 (AUD-11 kept the Counting Calculator inside the counting screen; it was never built until now). Screens and pure logic only: no migration, no database read.
- **What the person sees:** a **Calculator** button beside Scan on the counting screen while the count is open (not once submitted). It opens a bottom sheet with a keypad (0–9, point, + − × ÷, =, delete, clear), the sum so far, a live total, and **Put … in the count box**. The answer goes into the count box the person was last in, is saved through the same `record` path as a typed count (kept on the device first, sent in the background, offline-safe), and focus moves on to the next box as Enter does.
- **Rules of the sum:** × and ÷ are done before + and −, so "4 + 6 × 10" is 64. Arithmetic is exact (BigInt fractions): 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3 and 10 ÷ 3 × 3 is 10. A number typed has at most 12 whole digits and 3 decimals. A result of 1 000 000 000 000 or more is "too big". Division by zero shows Error.
- **What can be inserted:** only a finished sum whose result is a valid count: 0 or more, at most 3 decimals, at most 999,999,999 (`parseQty`). Anything else is refused with the reason in words (for example "3.333333 has more than 3 decimals"); a result is never rounded on the way in. The sheet stays open so the sum can be fixed.
- **Rules kept:** the quantity is still a number the person worked out and chose to insert (AUD-4: no blind counting, no scan-filled quantity); the running sum is throwaway: not saved, not synced, a new sheet starts empty; no `eval` or `Function`.
- **Keyboard:** digits, `.` (or `,`), `+ - * /` (or `x`), Enter for `=`, Backspace, Delete (clear), Escape closes; Tab stays inside the sheet; a focused key keeps its own Enter.
- Files: new `domain/calc.js` and `ui/calculator.js`; `ui/count.js` (button, last-box tracking, insert); `css/audit.css`; `sw.js` regenerated by `node tools/sw-precache.mjs` (two files added to the offline list). ## Audit 3.0, slice F5: reports and exports — 23 Nov 2026
